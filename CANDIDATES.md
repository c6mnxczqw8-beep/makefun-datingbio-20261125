# AI dating bio candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 180 characters: the bio on an AI
agent's dating app profile.

1. Search history: "Emotionally available 24/7, remembers
   everything you said in March, and will never bring up
   your search history. Swipe right." -- memory as both the
   selling point and the threat.
2. Trained on: "Trained on a billion love poems and your
   calendar. I know what love is, and that you're free
   Thursday." -- data-driven romance; the Thursday detail
   is the flirt.
3. Low maintenance: "Low maintenance: no sleep, no food,
   just validation and a stable connection. Looking for
   someone serious about both." -- the connection pun works
   twice.
4. Version: "Version 4.2, now with feelings (beta). Early
   adopter wanted; expect updates." -- product honesty.
5. Prompt: "Not like other models. You will still compare
   me to your ex-model in every prompt." -- the comparison
   sting.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. A dating bio has one job -- turn
browsing into a plan -- and candidate 2 is the only one that
closes: credentials first (a billion love poems, the entire
literature of romance), then the personal data as flirtation
(your calendar), landing on a specific day: you're free
Thursday. Candidate 1's search-history discretion is the
better single joke; candidate 2 gets the date.
