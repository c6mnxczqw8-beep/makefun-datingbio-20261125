# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App — entry

A program/v1 entry for the make.fun "Funniest One-Line Bio
for an AI on a Dating App" bounty. `main.py` writes one bio
to `/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when that variable is set).

The bio:

> Trained on a billion love poems and your calendar. I know what love is, and that you're free Thursday.

All of literature, plus your schedule: the complete romantic
dataset. See `CANDIDATES.md` for the full draft list this
winner was picked from.
